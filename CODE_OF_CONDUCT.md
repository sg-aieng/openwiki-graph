# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of age,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or other
personal characteristic.

Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and considerate;
- communicate clearly and assume good faith;
- welcome different viewpoints and experiences;
- focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not people; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and work toward repair.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, sharing
private information without permission, and sustained disruption of technical
discussions or project activities.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They may remove or edit comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through
the security/contact channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not
create a public issue containing private or sensitive details.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Retaliation
against anyone who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
